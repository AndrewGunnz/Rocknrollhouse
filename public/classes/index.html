Muay Thai

Adults

Striking with fists, elbows, knees and shins. All Levels classes are technical, fitness-focused and non-contact. Advanced adds pad work, partner drills and controlled sparring, and Sparring is supervised rounds for students ready to test their skills.

  • All Levels
  • Advanced
  • Sparring
See Muay Thai times

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Adults

Grappling and self-defense in a controlled, supportive setting. Gi classes train in a clean gi; No-Gi trains in a rash guard and focuses on control, submissions and transitions. Suitable for beginners and experienced grapplers alike.

  • Gi, All Levels
  • No-Gi, All Levels
See BJJ times

Onna-musha women's BJJ

Women

A focused training space for women to build skill, strength and self-reliance through Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, with a strong emphasis on technique and safety. Coached by Rebecca Contrino.

See Onna-musha times

Teen Spirit

Teens

Muay Thai and Brazilian Jiu Jitsu classes just for teens. The same technical coaching as the adult classes, built around coordination, endurance and confidence.

  • Muay Thai
  • Brazilian Jiu Jitsu
See teen class times

Kids intro to MMA

Ages 4 to 11

Striking and grappling in a fun, age-appropriate way, with an emphasis on movement, discipline and problem-solving. Two groups: ages 4 to 7, and Future Champions for ages 8 to 11. Kids classes can fill up, so book ahead.

  • Ages 4 to 7
  • Future Champions, 8 to 11
