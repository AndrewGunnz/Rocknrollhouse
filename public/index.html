Muay Thai

Adults

Striking with fists, elbows, knees and shins. All Levels classes are technical, fitness-focused and non-contact. Advanced adds pad work, partner drills and controlled sparring, and Sparring is supervised rounds for students ready to test their skills.

  • All Levels
  • Advanced
  • Sparring
Find a Muay Thai class

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu

Adults

Grappling and self-defense in a controlled, supportive setting. Gi classes train in a clean gi; No-Gi trains in a rash guard and focuses on control, submissions and transitions. Suitable for beginners and experienced grapplers alike.

  • Gi, All Levels
  • No-Gi, All Levels

Onna-musha women's BJJ

Women

A focused training space for women to build skill, strength and self-reliance through Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, with a strong emphasis on technique and safety. Coached by Rebecca Contrino.

Muay Thai and Brazilian Jiu Jitsu classes just for teens. The same technical coaching as the adult classes, built around coordination, endurance and confidence.

New here? How your first class works

Your first class

No experience needed. Here's how to get on the mats.

  1. Pick a class

    Choose any class marked All Levels in the schedule. Not sure which style fits? Call 438 535 6855.

  2. Book it on Mindbody

    Tap Book, enter your email to sign in or create your Mindbody account, then check out. A drop-in is $40 plus tax.

  3. Show up ready

    Wear training clothes and bring water, a towel and a mouth guard. Muay Thai uses hand wraps and gloves; Gi classes need a clean gi.

    Missing gear? The gym shop stocks gloves, wraps, mouth guards and gis.

More than a place to train

At Rock 'N' Roll House, the sound of striking gloves and bare feet on mats echoes louder than any amplifier. This is a space where the timeless values of martial arts meet the rhythm and energy of rock 'n' roll: raw, honest and deeply personal.

People come here to build physical strength, mental focus and emotional resilience. With every class, students of all levels develop the tools to move with purpose, fight with intention, and carry themselves with confidence inside and outside the gym.

We honour the old-school mindset:

  • Respect for the craft
  • Discipline in the process
  • Humility in learning
  • Courage in challenge
