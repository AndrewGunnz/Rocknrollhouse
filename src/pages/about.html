About the house

Where the timeless values of martial arts meet the rhythm and energy of rock and roll.

More than a place to train

At Rock 'N' Roll House, the sound of striking gloves and bare feet on mats echoes louder than any amplifier. This is a space where the timeless values of martial arts meet the rhythm and energy of rock 'n' roll: raw, honest and deeply personal.

People come here to build physical strength, mental focus and emotional resilience. With every class, students of all levels develop the tools to move with purpose, fight with intention, and carry themselves with confidence inside and outside the gym.

We honour the old-school mindset:

  • Respect for the craft
  • Discipline in the process
  • Humility in learning
  • Courage in challenge
