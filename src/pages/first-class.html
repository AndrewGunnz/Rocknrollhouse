Your first class

No experience needed. Here's how to get on the mats.

  1. Pick a class

    Choose any class marked All Levels in the schedule. Not sure which style fits? Call 438 535 6855.

  2. Book it on Mindbody

    Tap Book, enter your email to sign in or create your Mindbody account, then check out. A drop-in is $40 plus tax.

  3. Show up ready

    Wear training clothes and bring water, a towel and a mouth guard. Muay Thai uses hand wraps and gloves; Gi classes need a clean gi.

    Missing gear? The gym shop stocks gloves, wraps, mouth guards and gis.

Good to know

Which class should I start with?
Any class marked All Levels: Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu Jitsu (Gi or No-Gi) or Onna-musha for women. Teens and kids have their own classes. See all classes.
How much is it?
A drop-in is $40 plus tax. If you join a membership afterwards, you get $40 off your first month. See memberships.
Do I need my own gear?
Bring a mouth guard, water and a towel. Each class lists its gear in the schedule under Show Details. The gym shop stocks gloves, wraps, mouth guards and gis.
